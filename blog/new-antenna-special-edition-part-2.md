## Gain Optimization

Adjusting the gain was not straightforward.

- Too high → saturation from strong nearby signals, reducing long-range reception  
- Too low → overall reception drops, affecting ranking performance  

After repeated testing and fine-tuning,  
I settled on **44.5 dB** as the optimal value.
