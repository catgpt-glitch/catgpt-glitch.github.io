## Reflection vs Diffraction

| Feature        | Reflection        | Diffraction           |
|----------------|------------------|----------------------|
| Position       | Shifted          | Along shadow region  |
| Signal Strength| Sometimes strong | Weak but consistent  |
| Pattern        | Point-like       | Area-like            |
| This case      | ❌               | ✅                   |

---

👉 In this case,  
the signal reappears **within the shadow region**.

→ This strongly suggests **diffraction**

---

## Conceptual Model

- Route A → Clear line-of-sight  
- Route B → Edge interaction (signal loss)  
- Route C → Diffraction zone (signal recovery)

The behavior observed along Route B strongly resembles a diffraction pattern rather than simple reflection.  
The signal disappears near the obstacle and reappears behind it, consistent with edge diffraction.

---

## 🐈 Summary

- Strong reception to the west → Terrain effects  
- Route B → Likely diffraction  
- NE anomaly → Possibly a different mechanism (reflection or abnormal propagation)
